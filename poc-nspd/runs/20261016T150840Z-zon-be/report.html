Inside the overdruk-marked area 'gebied voor de productie van hernieuwbare energie' of GRUP zeehavengebied Gent - fase 2, all works for constructing, operating and adapting renewable-energy infrastructure are allowed; the underlying ground-colour destination applies insofar as renewable-energy infrastructure is not compromised — covers solar fields as renewable-energy infrastructure.

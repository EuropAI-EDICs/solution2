# NLDT Q3 2026 Quarterly Report – Summary in English (App Store Focus)

---

## 📌 Executive Summary: NLDT Program & App Store Ambitions

The **Network of Local Digital Twins (NLDT)** program—led by the Dutch **Ministry of the Interior and Kingdom Relations (BZK)**—aims to **accelerate spatial transition challenges** by improving the reuse of accessible, intelligent spatial information in work processes. A **core pillar** of this mission is the **development of a national App Store infrastructure** to scale modular, interoperable applications for digital twins (DTs) across government, businesses, and knowledge institutions.

**Strategic Goal:**
✅ Scale proven DT solutions (from field labs) via a **modular App Store** with national focus on **interoperability standards** and **validated calculation models** (e.g., for housing, energy, water, and defense sectors).

---

The App Store is embedded in NLDT’s **"Scaling and Adoption" strategy** and serves as the **technical backbone** for:

 |-------------------------|---------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------|

## 📈 Key App Store Developments (Q3 2026)

### 🚀 Progress & Milestones

- **App Store Realization**:
  - **Status**: *Ongoing* (target: 2028).
  - **2026 Preparations**: NLDT **testbed** launched to develop **"DT recipes"** (standardized templates for orchestrating calculation models).
  - **Organizational Models**: Research into **public-private partnerships** for a **national App Store**, including the **role of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management (VRO)**.
  - **Compliance**: Collaboration with **PDX working group** to align with NLDT **reference architecture** (compliance checks, knowledge exchange).

- **Field Labs & Use Cases**:
  - **10+ new DT cases** identified (e.g., **Zeewolde military base**, **IJssel-Vecht Delta water/soil domain**).
  - **Housing Sector**: Taskforce for **accelerating housing construction** proposed a **network of digital twins**, with App Store infrastructure as a key enabler (funding request submitted to the minister in Q3).
  - **Provincial/Municipal Adoption**: **30 municipalities** joined the **TBGO collaboration agenda** (e.g., **Eindhoven, Overijssel, MRA**).

- **Calculation Models**:
  - **WUR (Wageningen University & Research)** drafted the **"Agenda for Proper Use of Calculation Models"** to improve **quality, trust, and reuse** of models in DTs.
  - **App Store** will host a **catalog of validated models** (e.g., for climate adaptation, energy, housing).

## 🎯 Strategic Takeaways (App Store Emphasis)

1. **App Store as Scaling Engine**:
   The **modular App Store** is the **primary vehicle** for scaling NLDT’s **digital twin solutions** from pilot projects (field labs) to **national adoption**, with a focus on **interoperability standards** and **validated calculation models**.

2. **Funding Urgency**:
   **2027 is the critical year** for App Store realization (€5.58M investment), but **€5.248M remains unfunded**. Success depends on:
   - **Housing Taskforce fiche** (€34.8M for 2027–2030).
   - **EU EDIC contributions** (e.g., joint marketplace project).
   - **Structural budget** from 2029 onward (€4.5M/year).

3. **Governance & Standards**:
   - **Geonovum** will manage the **reference architecture** (annual updates).
   - **DT recipes** will standardize **orchestration between calculation models** (hosted in App Store).
   - **Compliance checks** (via PDX working group) to ensure alignment with NLDT standards.

4. **Community & Adoption**:
   - **30+ municipalities** and **multiple sectors** (housing, defense, water) are **target users** of App Store modules.
   - **Workshops** (e.g., "Bekijk mijn wijk") and **EU events** (Barcelona Expo) will drive **visibility and collaboration**.

5. **Long-Term Vision**:
   By 2030, the App Store aims to **embed digital twins in Dutch spatial planning workflows**, making them **accessible, interoperable, and reusable** across all government levels.

the function as the shared level

A demonstration of the reference implementation for urban planning, built on the Finnish research into the Agentic State (Sitra, September 2026) — and on six already working proof-of-concepts from the LDT toolbox that show the approach is possible today.

The insight

More AI pilots is not yet renewal. The question is not which tool, but at what level you understand, compare and redesign public work: the function.

The reference implementation

One shared function — preparing a spatial assessment — running from question to a legally grounded dossier, with validation gates and a learning loop.

1 · The diagnosis

More pilots is not yet renewal

Finland's Sitra produced the world's first nationwide readiness mapping of 70 recurring government functions across 109 organisations for agentic AI. The finding: organisations automate isolated tasks inside their own silos — while often redesigning the same work separately.

Organisation-first

Municipalityown intake process
Provinceown intake process
Water boardown intake process

The same work designed and procured three times over; information requested repeatedly; total potential invisible.

Function-first

ReceiveCheckPrepareNotifyPay

Recurring work becomes visible across organisations: what is shared, what stays organisation-specific, what do we build together?

Agentic AI makes this urgent. Agents can carry out bounded, multi-step work across system and organisational boundaries — which makes redesigning the whole function (rather than speeding up one task) suddenly feasible. (Sitra, 2026)

Source: Sitra working paper §1 · First Moves assessment (70 functions × 109 organisations).

    5 · The executive investment gate

    Five gates before technology becomes a productivity investment

    Sitra's hard precondition: redesign the work first, procure later. The toolbox already has three gates in place as technical validation (V0–V4); two gates are new and become first-class artifacts.

    1

    Outcome & work removed

    Baseline of cost, lead time, errors — and: which work disappears?

    new: function-baseline.json
    2

    Shared meaning

    Authoritative source + owner per concept; a shared vocabulary across organisations.

    ≈ V2 grounding · norm cards with citation ✓
    3

    Legal & human boundary

    What may the agent do, when does it escalate, who stays responsible, how does a human correct?

    ≈ V2/V4 · cite-or-abstain + HITL ✓
    4

    Replaceable architecture

    Stable interfaces around durable concepts; models and suppliers stay replaceable.

    ✓ open source, local models, contracts
    5

    Evidence & stop conditions

    Agreed in advance: which evidence triggers continue, change, pause or stop?

    new: stop conditions in run reporting

    The doctrine in one sentence: no citation, no rule; no human, no decision. A technology demonstration that cannot answer the five gates is a trial, not yet a productivity demonstrator. (Sitra §4.5)

    6 · Reuse & scale

    Different services, one reusable core

    Distinct services — stay separate

    permit preparation Utrecht · zoning-plan conversion Eindhoven · area scan Breda · water-level check Rijnland · area check MiniGIM — each with its own legal track and its own user.

    Reusable core — what is genuinely common

    intake normalisation · norm harvesting with citation · formalisation into executable rules · zone algebra · independent validation · provenance (PROV) · decision table & explanation. Standardise what is shared, make variation explicit.

    Multiple interoperable applications & suppliers

    an open specification (recipes / schemas / MCP tools) lets multiple suppliers serve the same core — without one dominant vendor or system.

    Shared public infrastructure

    BAG · BGT · DSO/CVDR · EU LDT Toolbox (Data Platform, Identity, Data Space) — foundations beneath all functions.

    RegelRecht: the first local contributor

    The national corpus holds ~22,000 machine-readable statutes — not yet a single municipal or provincial ordinance. The Eindhoven and Utrecht exports are technically validated against the national schema: the first contribution is ready.

    Inside your own organisation

    Eindhoven is building a knowledge bank of 274 official conversions: every subsequent plan amendment faster and more consistent. Utrecht reuses one computation chain for three policy tracks.

    7 · The learning state

    From signal to decision — the loop pilots lack

    A successful run is the beginning. The question is whether evidence from the human–agent interplay also changes the work, the roles and the rules — in your own organisation and in others performing the same function.

    Capture signals

    overrides, exceptions, delays, correction requests, unequal outcomes — past runs and PROV are the raw feed; the journal already exists.

    Decision trail (new)

    what happened → why → what should change → who decides → did it help? Five questions, mandatorily recorded for every material signal.

    Three learning levels

    operational (step/prompt) · organisational (role/workflow) · institutional (standard/funding/regulation) — with an explicit escalation route.

    Opposite risks (Sitra): experimentation without shared learning leaves evidence trapped in projects; governance without learning freezes rules around processes that themselves need to change. Hence: the learning loop is a phase-exit, not an after-the-fact report.

    Source: Sitra §5 · docs/AGENTIC_STATE_PLAN.md B10.

    8 · The path

    Eighteen months as a bounded learning period

    Not a race to a national platform, but one demonstrator that tests the proposition — with decision points agreed in advance: continue, change, pause or stop.

    mo 0–3

    A · Catalogue & readiness map

    10–20 spatial-planning functions with scores and source quotes; instance inventory (incl. the Omgevingschat platform); demonstrator function chosen.

    exit: choice with rationale
    mo 2–5

    B · Baseline & vocabulary

    function baseline per organisation; governed vocabulary (Location Item ↔ NormCard); steward + participation agreement.

    exit: gates 1 + 2 passed
    mo 4–8

    C · Redesign & boundary

    workflows before technology; human–agent boundary per step; HITL interrupts (bp2op first); gates 3 + 4 in the critic.

    exit: five gates open
    mo 6–12

    D · Demonstrator ≥ 3 organisations

    runs with gates & PROV; evidence: overrides, delay, completeness; golden sets; evidence reviews at decision points.

    exit: continue/stop on evidence
    mo 10–18

    E · Learn & package

    decision trail; reusable core as open specification (recipes/skills/MCP); stewardship proposal and the 2030 questions.

    exit: reference implementation documented

    9 · What we ask of the board

    What we ask

    1. Adopt the working method as the standard

      for AI assistance on law and regulation: no citation, no rule; no human, no decision.

    2. Co-decide: one function, three organisations

      confirm "preparing a spatial assessment" as the demonstrator function and join as a participating organisational context (Utrecht · Breda · Eindhoven · Rijnland are already in the toolbox).

    3. Give the learning period a mandate

      18 months bounded, with a function steward, baseline before technology, and stop conditions agreed in advance — progress judged on evidence, not promise.

    4. Contribute the instruments publicly

      Eindhoven's environment plan and Utrecht's ordinance as the first municipal and provincial contributions to the national RegelRecht corpus.

    One function. One reusable core. One eighteen-month learning period.

    Status — honestly: all six PoCs run fully offline and repeatably with independent validation per run. Still open: the full instrument export, the live RegelRecht connection, HITL interrupts in the orchestrator, and the function catalogue itself (phase A). Deliberately not: AI that decides.
